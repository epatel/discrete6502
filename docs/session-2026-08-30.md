## 2. What was established today, with the evidence

Ordered by how much weight it will carry for you.

**(a) The Pico is healthy, and so are the cable, the port and the rail at rest.**
`SELFTEST_USB_ONLY` — a build that calls `stdio_init_all()` and touches nothing else — printed
**91 lines over 45 s with a rock-stable link**. Any explanation that starts "the Pico is damaged"
has to get past this.

**(b) Driving `clk0` low is instantly fatal; leaving it undriven is indefinitely fine.**
Three builds, each removing one variable:

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| nothing driven | 91 lines / 45 s — stable |
| `bus_init(open_drain=true)` — 22 pins to inputs, `clk0` left an input | 8.5 s, 17 prints — healthy |
| drive `clk0` LOW, push-pull | **dead 230 ms later** |
| drive `clk0` LOW for **1 ms**, then release | **dead** |

**(c) There is a hole in yesterday's `clk0` model, and it is the live lead.**
`docs/clk0-pulldown.md` traced `clk0` → `cclk` and concluded "clk0 low is the quiet state". It
**never traced `cp1`**. Traced now: `clk0` LOW → `n358` HIGH → `n1715` LOW → `n1399` HIGH →
Q2482 on → **`cp1` HIGH**. The phases are complementary, `cp1` gates **198 loads**, and in a 6502
φ1 is when the pass gates conduct. So `clk0` LOW turns 32 precharge FETs *off* and 198 gates *on*,
and only the first half was ever checked. **Every firmware currently parks `clk0` LOW on the
strength of that incomplete trace.**

**(d) The 16 remaining precharge sites are quantified.**
`tools/contention_duty.py --halves 600`: `idb0-7` and `sb0-7` contend **14.7–16.0%** of the time
under real code — about half the address path's 34%, but *above* several of the `dor` eight that
were reworked first. Sixteen sites at 15% ≈ 2.5 nets contending continuously ≈ **0.65 A**.
**Q1830 is `idb7`**, the part the user's thermal camera picked out on 2026-08-29 — model and camera
agree on where to start.

**(e) A 31 kΩ pull-down is too weak, and 47 kΩ (my earlier advice) would have been worse.**
Measured: `clk0` at **0.77 V ⇒ 0.24 A**, and **1.76 V ⇒ 2.5 A**, straddling the 0.8–1.5 V Vgs(th)
band. That means **25–57 µA is being sourced into the node**, where the document assumed the only
load was two FET gates drawing nothing. At that leak 31 kΩ gives 1.76 V and 47 kΩ would give ~2.7 V
— fully on. **4.7 kΩ is the right value**: 0.12–0.27 V parked, still 2.72 V driven.
**What sources the 25–57 µA is not known** and is an open question.

## 3. The contradiction that is still unresolved

**Undriven, `clk0` sits at 0.77 V — below the 0.8 V threshold, logically identical to ground — and
the board draws 0.24 A. Driving that same pin to ground kills the module in 1 ms.** Those two
states should be electrically equivalent. They are not, and no explanation offered today survives.

Eliminated as explanations:

- **The Mac cutting the port on overcurrent** — macOS logs show *no* overcurrent and no "device
  disabled" for the whole session.
- **A pin-mapping error** — checked: GP22 → physical pin 29 → `clk0`, and all 26 signals land on
  real GPIOs, none on a rail or a non-GPIO pin.
- **A short on the node** — the user's unpowered readings (9.5 kΩ to VSS, 8 kΩ to VCC) are diode
  paths of the kind documented on 2026-08-12, not a fault.
- **"1 ms is too fast for a sagging rail"** — *withdrawn*, this was my error. The Pico carries
  ~10 µF on VSYS, which at 2.5 A collapses in microseconds, so a 1 ms current step is ample.

## 6. Traps that cost time today — do not re-pay them

- **The serial port renames.** It was `/dev/cu.usbmodem1101` and became `/dev/cu.usbmodem11101`.
  **Glob, never hard-code.** A hard-coded reader silently found nothing and looked like a dead board.
- **Copy UF2s with `cp`, not `shutil.copy`.** Copying xattrs onto the FAT bootloader volume fails
  with EPERM *after the data is written* — it reads as an error and is not.
- **Do not attach a terminal to the tester firmware while measuring.** It stops clocking on attach.
- **Do not poll a long run over wifi** — lwIP wedges, seen at one poll per minute. Read the verdict
  on USB serial.
- **Never write Pico flash while the CPU runs** — it stalls the core including lwIP.
- **The navigator: use `http://127.0.0.1:8624/` for annotations.** The deployed copy at
  `ai.memention.net/d6502navigator/` is read-only unless you pass `?key=<token>`, and the token
  lives only in `/etc/d6502navigator.env` on the `ai` VPS.
- **`navctl.py` has no `group` subcommand** — groups are a server endpoint:
  `curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8624/api/group -d '{"id":"precharge-idb"}'`.
- **Withdrawn as unsafe: powering the whole board from USB alone.** The board current would pass
  through the Pico's VBUS→VSYS Schottky, a part sized for 500 mA, not 2.5 A. The Mac is fine — it
  current-limits — but that diode is not, and losing it kills the module.
